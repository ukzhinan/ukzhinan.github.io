在英国，简历（CV）和求职信（Cover Letter）是求职成功的第一关。和中国不同，英国雇主更看重**简洁、针对性和实际成就**，而不是长篇的工作经历罗列。对于兼职岗位，更重要的是展示你能快速融入岗位并胜任职责。


<h2 id="sec-1">英文简历编写原则</h2>

1. **一页原则**
    - 兼职简历通常一页即可，突出与岗位相关的经历。
        
2. **量化成果**
    - 用数字说明你的成就，例如“Handled transactions for over 50 customers daily”。
        
3. **关键词匹配**
    - 仔细阅读岗位描述（Job Description），将其中的关键词融入简历内容。
        
4. **倒序排列**
    - 最近的教育与工作经历放在最前面。


<h2 id="sec-2">针对不同兼职的简历模板</h2>

**① 餐饮零售类简历**（Customer Service / Retail CV）

```text
[Your Name]  
[UK Address] | [Phone] | [Email]  

Personal Statement  
Friendly and efficient MSc student at [University Name], with proven experience in customer service and cash handling. Skilled in working in fast-paced environments while maintaining high levels of customer satisfaction.

Experience  
Barista – Starbucks, Shanghai, China (Jun 2023 – Aug 2023)  
- Prepared and served beverages to over 100 customers daily.  
- Managed cash register and maintained accurate transactions.  
- Ensured cleanliness and compliance with health and safety standards.  

Skills  
- Customer service, teamwork, time management  
- Languages: Mandarin (Native), English (Fluent)

```


**② 校内岗位简历**（Student Ambassador / Library Assistant CV）

```text
Personal Statement  
Organised and approachable MSc student at [University Name], experienced in event coordination and administrative support. Passionate about representing the university and assisting fellow students.

Experience  
Student Ambassador – [University Name] (Oct 2024 – Present)  
- Assisted in campus tours for prospective students and parents.  
- Supported open day events with over 500 visitors.  
- Handled enquiries via email and in person.

Skills  
- Event organisation, public speaking, Microsoft Office  
- Languages: Mandarin (Native), English (Fluent)

```


**③ 专业技能类简历**（Tutor / Graphic Designer CV）

```text
Personal Statement  
Detail-oriented Computer Science student at [University Name], with strong skills in Python, data analysis, and tutoring. Experienced in delivering customised learning sessions for students of varying skill levels.

Experience  
Math Tutor – Private (Remote & In-person) (2023 – Present)  
- Delivered 1-on-1 sessions for GCSE and A-Level students.  
- Improved student grades by an average of 20% over one term.  

Skills  
- Python, Excel, Adobe Photoshop  
- Languages: Mandarin (Native), English (Fluent)
```



<h2 id="sec-3">针对不同兼职的求职信模板</h2>

**① 餐饮零售类 Cover Letter**

```text
Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the Part-time Barista position at [Company Name]. As a current MSc student at [University Name], I bring strong customer service skills and experience working in fast-paced environments.

In my previous role at Starbucks in Shanghai, I served over 100 customers daily while ensuring consistent quality and maintaining hygiene standards. I am confident my ability to work efficiently and communicate effectively will make me a valuable member of your team.

I am available to work evenings and weekends, and I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute.

Yours sincerely,  
[Your Name]

```


**② 校内岗位 Cover Letter**

```text
Dear [Hiring Manager’s Name],

I am writing to express my interest in the Student Ambassador position at [University Name]. I am enthusiastic about representing the university and assisting prospective students in their decision-making process.

As a current MSc student, I have volunteered at open day events, guided campus tours, and provided administrative support to event teams. These experiences have developed my organisational skills and my ability to engage with people from diverse backgrounds.

I would welcome the chance to bring my energy and skills to this role.

Kind regards,  
[Your Name]

```


**③ 专业技能类 Cover Letter**

```text
Dear [Client/Employer Name],

I am applying for the part-time Tutor position advertised on [Platform]. With a strong academic background in [Subject] and over two years of tutoring experience, I am confident in my ability to deliver high-quality learning support.

I have successfully helped students improve their exam results by developing personalised study plans and providing targeted practice. My approach combines patience, clarity, and real-world examples to keep students engaged.

I look forward to discussing how I can contribute to your learning goals.

Yours sincerely,  
[Your Name]

```
