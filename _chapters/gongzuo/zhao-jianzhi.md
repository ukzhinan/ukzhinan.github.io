在英国找兼职的核心，不只是“投简历”，而是一个从信息获取、简历准备、面试跟进到入职确认的**完整流程**。如果你是刚到英国的新生，建议先用1-2周熟悉环境，同时准备好求职材料，再开始系统性寻找兼职机会。


<h2 id="sec-1">求职流程 SOP（适合初来英国留学生）</h2>

1. **信息收集（Week 1-2）**
    
    - 关注学校 Career Service、Job Shop、学生会的招聘公告
    - 在招聘平台（Indeed、Reed、Gumtree、StudentJob）设置岗位提醒
    - 加入本地 Facebook 租房/兼职群 & 华人微信群（但要谨防诈骗）
        
2. **简历 & 求职信准备（Week 1-2）**
    
    - 准备英文简历（CV）+ Cover Letter 模板
    - 不同岗位调整简历关键词（如餐饮岗位突出 customer service，校内岗位突出 organisational skills）
    - 预留推荐人（References）——可以是导师、实习上司或国内前雇主
        
3. **投递与跟进（Week 2-4）**
    
    - 每周固定2-3天集中投递（效率更高，便于跟进）
    - 投递后一周无回复，可礼貌跟进（电话或邮件）
    - 对于本地商铺/咖啡馆，可直接带纸质简历上门询问
        
4. **面试准备（Week 3-5）**
    
    - 熟悉常见面试问题（Tell me about yourself / Why do you want to work here?）
    - 模拟2-3分钟自我介绍，突出语言简洁+微笑+眼神交流
    - 了解雇主背景（官网、Google 评价）
        
5. **Offer确认 & 入职（Week 4-6）**
    
    - 确认合同（Contract）、时薪、工作时间、休假规则
    - 提供 NI Number（国民保险号） & 银行账户
    - 保存合同副本和工资单（Payslip）——未来续签/留英可能需要


**英文简历（CV）结构模板**

```text
[Your Name]  
[UK Address] | [Phone] | [Email] | [LinkedIn URL]  

Personal Statement  
A motivated and reliable postgraduate student at [University Name], specialising in [Your Major]. Skilled in [Skill 1], [Skill 2], with proven experience in [Relevant Experience]. Seeking part-time opportunities to contribute to [Employer’s Goal] while further developing professional skills.

Education  
[University Name], [City], UK  
MSc in [Major], Expected [Month Year]  
Key Modules: [Module 1], [Module 2], [Module 3]  

Experience  
Customer Service Assistant – [Company Name], [City], [Month Year – Month Year]  
- Delivered excellent customer service to diverse clients, resolving inquiries efficiently.  
- Managed cash transactions with 100% accuracy.  

Skills  
- Languages: Mandarin (Native), English (Fluent)  
- IT: Microsoft Office, POS system, [Other Tools]  
- Soft Skills: Time management, teamwork, problem-solving  

References  
Available upon request.

```

**英文求职信（Cover Letter）简化模板**

```text
Dear [Hiring Manager’s Name],

I am writing to express my interest in the [Job Title] position at [Company Name], as advertised on [Platform/Source]. As a current MSc student in [Your Major] at [University Name], I have developed strong [Skill 1] and [Skill 2] skills, which I believe align closely with the requirements of this role.

During my previous experience as [Position] at [Company], I [Achievement/Responsibility 1] and [Achievement/Responsibility 2]. These experiences have strengthened my ability to [Relevant Skill] and to work effectively in a [Team/Customer-focused] environment.

I am enthusiastic about contributing my skills to [Company Name] and am confident that my background and work ethic would make me a valuable addition to your team. I am available to work [State Your Availability], and I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute.

Thank you for considering my application.

Yours sincerely,  
[Your Name]

```

<h2 id="sec-2">常用招聘网站 & APP推荐</h2>

| 平台                | 类型     | 特点             | 适合岗位     |
| ----------------- | ------ | -------------- | -------- |
| **Indeed**        | 综合招聘平台 | 覆盖面广，搜索功能强     | 所有类型     |
| **Reed**          | 综合招聘平台 | 职位描述详尽，过滤功能好   | 办公/服务    |
| **StudentJob**    | 学生专属招聘 | 兼职、实习多         | 校内/灵活兼职  |
| **Gumtree Jobs**  | 本地分类信息 | 可按城市筛选，适合上门递简历 | 餐饮零售     |
| **LinkedIn**      | 职业社交平台 | 建立专业形象，人脉资源丰富  | 专业型/实习   |
| **Upwork/Fiverr** | 自由职业平台 | 技术类远程项目多       | 设计/编程/翻译 |
